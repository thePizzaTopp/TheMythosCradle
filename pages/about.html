Adopt A Creature

Your journey with a little legend begins here

Adopting a little legend costs nothing—our priority is finding them loving homes. To make their arrival extra special, you can choose optional starter packages designed specifically for your creature, including tiny beds, treats, toys, and more. You’re welcome to select multiple packages if your legend will have both indoor and outdoor spaces. Once you’ve completed your application, we’ll arrange a magical meeting to help you discover which creature is the perfect match. We’ve also included a beginner-friendly rating for each creature, so you can decide if you’re ready to welcome them into your family.
